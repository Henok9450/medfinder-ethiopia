Strict EFDA Compliance Rule

To prevent counterfeit medicine listings, pharmacies cannot register with simple email/passwords. You must verify your facility through the official @MedFinder_Verifier_bot.

  1. Open @MedFinder_Verifier_bot on Telegram on your phone or desktop.
  2. Type /start verify.
  3. Tap "📍 Share Live GPS Location" from your pharmacy counter.
  4. Upload a clear photo of your EFDA Pharmacist Competency Certificate.
  5. Your unique 1-Click Access Key will be delivered directly inside Telegram upon review.
